# Idea (shortlist draft, not locked)

**One line:** a live needs board for evacuation centers in the Bicol Region, where center managers post what they need (goods or volunteer hands) and donors and volunteers pledge to cover it.

**Who it serves**
- Evacuation center managers: parish social action centers, NGOs, barangay and LGU staff running centers in Albay (Mayon) and Camarines Sur (typhoons, Naga floods).
- Donors: individuals, schools, businesses, church groups who want to send the right things.
- Grassroots volunteers: student orgs, community pantries, parish youth.

**Core flow**
1. Manager posts the center's headcount and current needs. A need is goods (20 water jugs) or hands (5 volunteers to repack, Saturday 8am).
2. Donors pledge goods; volunteers claim shifts.
3. Manager marks items received and shifts done, so the board stays current.

**Why now:** Mayon 2023 kept about 20,000 people in crowded centers for months, with water and sanitation the main unmet need. Typhoon Kristine (2024) flooded about 30% of Naga City. Past responses show the bottleneck is matching and distribution, not supply.
