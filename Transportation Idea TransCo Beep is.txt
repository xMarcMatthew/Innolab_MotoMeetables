Transportation Idea: TransCo Beep ish idea:
- Pain points that the idea addresses:
	- Payment convenience
	- The fare question
	- Route trouble
	- Vehicle tracking approximation
- Main Novelty:
	- Digitalization of Payment for public transportation through a QR system or/and NFC Beep cards (Jeepneys, Tricycles, Vans, buses, etc.)

Main Features:
1. QR/Beep Card payment form - Offers a cashless mode of payment.
2. Fare computation - Computes your fair based on where you were picked up and where you dropped off.
3. Information dissemination for applicable vehicles (Jeepneys, Vans, Buses). - Provides information on where jeepney routes are. (ex. Centro to Pacol route, Centro-Magsaysay-Diversion route)
4. PUJ tracking for ETA. - Allows for waiting passengers to know where the nearest PUJ is and how long before they arrive. This allows them to make decision on how they would travel.
5. Commute guide - Provides information on how to travel from point A to Point B. Offers tips on what to ride, where to exit, and etc.

Who benefits and how?
1. Daily Commuters - Allows payments and computing for fares easier through an automated and digital computing system.
2. PUJ Operators - Takes away the inconvenience for payment transactions. (Computing changes, interacting with commuters while driving, etc.)
3. Travelers - Allows travelers to know where PUJ routes are, so they know where to go and wait.
